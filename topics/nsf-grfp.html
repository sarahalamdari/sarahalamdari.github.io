NSF Graduate Research Fellowship

I was supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship (NSF GRFP) during my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering at the University of Washington. The fellowship supported my research using enhanced sampling and molecular simulation to uncover the structure, function, and energetic driving forces of biomolecules at interfaces.

My doctoral thesis, Discovery of Biomolecular Structure–Function Mechanisms with Computational Frameworks at the Nanoscale, brought this work together (University of Washington, 2021).
