Molecular Simulation

During my Ph.D. I developed and applied enhanced sampling methods — metadynamics and parallel bias metadynamics — to probe the interfacial phenomena that govern biological processes at the microscale. This work combined molecular dynamics simulation with experiment to understand peptoid self-assembly, protein behavior at the air–water interface, and biomolecular adsorption to mineral surfaces.
