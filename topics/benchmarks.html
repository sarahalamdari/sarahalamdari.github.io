Benchmarks and Evaluation for Protein ML

Progress in protein machine learning depends on being able to measure it. I build benchmarks and evaluation tools that make protein models comparable, reproducible, and grounded in real-world design tasks — from fitness landscape prediction to protein–function annotation.
