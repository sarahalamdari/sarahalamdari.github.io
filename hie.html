Sarah Alamdari

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft Research, where I develop deep generative learning approaches for protein design to advance human health. My work sits at the intersection of biology, deep learning, and molecular simulation. Here is a curriculum vitae.

I build and apply tools that help us understand the fundamental driving forces in biological systems, so we can rationally design efficient bioinspired technologies — and dramatically increase the speed at which these molecules can be studied.

I completed my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering at the University of Washington as an NSF GRFP Fellow, where I developed enhanced sampling methods to probe interfacial phenomena governing biological processes at the microscale.

I am a first-generation college student from a Colombian/Iranian immigrant household, and I care deeply about equitable access and representation in computational research.
