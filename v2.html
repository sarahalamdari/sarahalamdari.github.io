I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft Research on the BioML team, where I build generative models for protein design to advance human health. I care about understanding the fundamental forces that drive biological systems, and using that understanding to rationally design efficient, bioinspired technologies, faster than we ever could before.

I completed my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering at the University of Washington, where I was an NSF GRFP Fellow. My research focused on developing enhanced sampling methods to probe the interfacial phenomena that govern biology at the microscale.

I care deeply about equitable access, mentorship, and representation in computational research, and about making the field more open to those underrepresented in it.
