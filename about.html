About Me

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft Research, where I develop deep generative learning approaches for protein design to advance human health.

My research sits at the intersection of biology, deep learning, and molecular simulation. I build and apply tools that help us understand the driving forces in biological systems, so we can rationally design efficient bioinspired technologies — and increase the speed at which these molecules can be studied.

I completed my PhD in Chemical Engineering at the University of Washington under Jim Pfaendtner as an NSF GRFP Fellow. There I developed enhanced sampling methods to probe interfacial phenomena governing biological processes at the microscale.

I am a first-generation college student from a Colombian/Iranian immigrant household, and I care deeply about equitable access and representation in computational research.
