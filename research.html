Research

I develop machine learning methods that design new, functional proteins — and the benchmarks that make protein ML measurable. My work builds on a foundation in molecular simulation and biophysics.

Generative models for protein design

Diffusion and language models that learn from evolutionary sequence and structure to generate new proteins — including EvoDiff, an evolutionary diffusion framework for protein generation, and the Dayhoff Atlas for scaling sequence diversity.

Benchmarks and evaluation for protein ML

Standardized benchmarks and tools that make protein machine learning measurable and reproducible, including FLIP2 for protein fitness landscapes and ProtNote for protein–function annotation.

Molecular simulation and biophysics

Enhanced sampling and molecular dynamics to uncover the structure, function, and energetics of biomolecules at interfaces — the foundation my generative work is built on.
